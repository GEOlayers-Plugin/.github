## 🚀 Overview
GEOlayers for Windows is an extension panel that runs inside Adobe After Effects and turns geographic data into fully editable, animatable map layers. Instead of exporting flat map images from another tool, you search for any place, pull in live map data, and design the look directly in your composition. Because GEOlayers for After Effects keeps everything as native layers and effects, you can key camera moves, reveal routes, and restyle the map at any point in your workflow. It is built for motion designers, editors, and journalists who need accurate, on-brand maps inside their existing After Effects projects.

## ✨ Features
- **Live map data** — Search a location and load real streets, borders, water, and labels as editable layers.
- **Custom map styling** — Control colors, line weights, typography, and layer visibility to match any brand or scene.
- **Animated camera moves** — Pan, zoom, and orbit across the map using keyframes just like any other After Effects layer.
- **Markers and routes** — Place points of interest, draw connecting paths, and animate reveals along a route.
- **Native composition integration** — Every element stays inside your project as standard layers, so effects and expressions work as expected.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Open an After Effects project and create or select the composition where your map will live.
2. Open the GEOlayers panel from the Window menu and search for the location you want to feature.
3. Add the map to your composition and adjust the style until it matches your scene.
4. Drop in markers or a route, then set keyframes to animate the camera and reveals.
5. Preview inside After Effects and render as part of your normal export.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**

**Is my data secure?**
GEOlayers works within your own After Effects project on your machine. Map searches use online map data, but your compositions, layers, and edits stay in your local project files under your control.
